Welcome.

This locally hosted website gives you a UX you can use to convert your raw footage files that you have collected from a dual action camera set-up, into single panoramic videos showing the entire filming area.

The process to go from the raw footage on the SD cards from your cameras requires three steps:

  1. Upload your raw footage from the SD cards to your chosen storage device, with the footage from the left camera going in the "Left Camera" directory, and the footage from the right camera going in the "Right Camera" directory.
  2. Group together the relevant files so that we have a single left camera and single right camera video per filming session.
  3. Stitch together the left and right camera footage into a single output video displaying the whole filming area.
