Welcome.

This locally hosted website gives you a UX you can use to convert your raw footage files that you have collected from a dual action camera set-up, into single panoramic videos showing the entire filming area.

The process requires three steps:

  1. Configure the paths to your left camera, right camera, and output directories in settings below. These can point directly to the SD cards in your cameras, or to a storage device if you prefer to copy footage across first.
  2. Optionally group together clips from the same session if your cameras split recordings into multiple chunks.
  3. Stitch together the left and right camera footage into a single output video displaying the whole filming area.

For more detailed instructions, visit the README.

For a brief video tutorial, visit here.

If you'd like to contribute to the project or report an issue, visit the GitHub repo.
